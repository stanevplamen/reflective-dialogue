# Article publishing

When asked to create, edit, or publish website articles, read and follow [ARTICLE_PUBLISHING.md](ARTICLE_PUBLISHING.md). Apply its editorial, SEO, image, accessibility, linking, and quality standards to article work. The source instructions currently available in `ARTICLE_PUBLISHING.md` end mid-sentence. Do not infer or fabricate the missing text.
